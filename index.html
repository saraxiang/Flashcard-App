🏇 Meet the Horse Breeds! 🏇

Arabian

Known for their distinctive head shape and high tail carriage. They're one of the oldest horse breeds!

🏇

Famous for racing! These athletic horses are bred for speed and agility.

🎠

Clydesdale

Gentle giants! These draft horses are known for their feathered feet and kind nature.
