Amazing Horse Facts! 🤔

👁️

Super Vision

Horses have almost 360-degree vision! They can see behind them without turning their heads.

💤

Standing Sleepers

Horses can sleep both lying down and standing up thanks to a special locking mechanism in their legs!

🧠

Smart Cookies

Horses can recognize human emotions and remember human friends after years of separation!

🏃‍♂️

Speed Demons

The fastest recorded horse speed is 55 mph! That's faster than most cars in the city.

🦷

Tooth Truth

You can tell a horse's age by looking at their teeth! They keep growing throughout their life.

❤️

Big Hearts

A horse's heart weighs about 8-10 pounds and pumps 8 gallons of blood per minute!
